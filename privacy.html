Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Creative Stone ("Creative Stone," "we," "us," or "our") operates thecreativestone.net (the "Site") and provides marketing, website, branding, and email services to businesses (the "Services"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit the Site or interact with us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the Site.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in a few ways:

  • Information you give us. When you submit our contact form, we collect your first and last name, email address, business type, the services you're interested in, and any details you choose to add. If you opt in, we also collect your consent to receive marketing emails.
  • Booking information. If you schedule a call through our Cal.com booking widget, Cal.com collects the details needed to book and manage that appointment (such as your name, email, and selected time), subject to Cal.com's own privacy policy.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we automatically receive certain technical information when you visit — your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring pages, and general usage patterns — through analytics cookies and similar technology.
  • Bot and spam prevention data. Our contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help confirm submissions come from real people rather than automated bots. Turnstile may process limited technical signals (such as browser and network characteristics) to do this.

We do not knowingly collect payment card numbers or other sensitive financial account details through the Site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiry, follow up on a submitted form, and schedule or manage calls;
  • Evaluate the fit and priority of an inquiry so we can respond appropriately;
  • Deliver, maintain, and improve the Services for our clients;
  • Send you the emails you've requested, including a receipt confirming we received your inquiry;
  • Send occasional marketing updates, but only if you've opted in — you can unsubscribe at any time;
  • Understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve its content, layout, and performance;
  • Detect, prevent, and address spam, abuse, fraud, or security issues; and
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies & Analytics

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) to remember your selections while browsing and to understand aggregate site usage through analytics. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the Site may not function as intended if you do.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on a small number of trusted vendors to run the Site and our Services. Each processes data on our behalf, under its own privacy and security practices:

  • PostHog — product analytics, to understand how visitors use the Site;
  • Cloudflare (Turnstile) — bot and spam protection on our contact form;
  • Resend — delivery of transactional emails, such as your inquiry receipt and our reply;
  • Cal.com — scheduling for booked calls;
  • Vercel — website hosting and infrastructure.

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't share it with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

5. How Long We Keep Information

We retain information for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, deliver the Services, maintain business records, and meet legal or accounting obligations. If you ask us to delete your information and we have no legal or legitimate business reason to keep it, we will.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to opt out of marketing communications. To make a request, use the contact form on our homepage and tell us what you'd like us to do. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in those emails, and doing so has no effect on transactional emails needed to respond to your inquiry or deliver a service you've requested.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for businesses and business decision-makers. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

9. International Visitors

The Site is hosted in the United States, and the vendors listed above may process information in the United States or other countries. By using the Site, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, which may have different data protection laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information? Reach us through the contact form on our homepage.
